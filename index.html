What Makes Revolutionary Chicken

REVOLUTIONARY?

New Ideas

From a chicken-controlled automatic door opener to composting right in the chicken run, Revolutionary Chicken is about coming up with and testing new ideas that will improve the lives of your chickens while making your life easier.

Made For You

Revolutionary Chicken focuses specifically on the needs of backyard chicken owners. While owners of large flocks can certainly benefit from these products and plans, they are designed for small flocks.

Affordable Pricing

Even though these ideas and products can make a huge difference, costs are kept to a minimum. Revolutionary Chicken will help you discover ways to make raising chickens both easy on your schedule and gentle on your pocketbook.
